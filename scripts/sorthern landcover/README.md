## Landcover Map For The Southern Part of Ghana 
The land cover map was developed as part of the Fostering Land Use Optimization for Watershed Sustainability (FLOWS) project, a joint initiative by NASA and USAID aimed at empowering developing regions with Earth observation and geospatial tools to enhance environmental decision-making. The primary goal of this classification is to monitor key land cover types, including forest cover, agriculture, and water bodies, to assess their impacts on watershed health and support sustainable land management practices.
The classification was based on Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 (HLS) imagery and PALSAR data. The image data used for classification represents the period between 2023 and 2024. The Random Forest (RF) algorithm was employed for the supervised classification. The classification resulted in the following land cover classes, which are listed along with their assigned values:
